# ProteinSplitAudit v0.2.0

ProteinSplitAudit v0.2.0 freezes a reproducible five-class E. coli K-12 pilot cohort and turns it
into random and cluster-aware Train/Validation/Test splits. The release records what was selected,
which sequences were grouped, how each split was allocated, and whether the Test-to-Train audits
found threshold-level leakage.

## Release contents

- clean regeneration of 2,632 UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot source records and 1,182 retained candidates;
- byte-identical candidate Parquet and FASTA compared with the historical v0.1 pilot;
- a 442-protein `pilot-v1` cohort across EC classes `2.7`, `3.1`, `1.1`, `2.1`, and `4.1`;
- 437 Cluster70, 427 Cluster50, and 398 Cluster30 strict components;
- Random, Cluster70, Cluster50, and Cluster30 splits, each with 308 Train, 68 Validation, and 66
  Test proteins;
- four independent Test-to-Train similarity audits;
- aggregate Split Summary and top-level provenance.

The three cluster-aware audits have zero violations at their named thresholds. The Random control
reports four observed hits at or above 30% identity, two at or above 50%, and none at or above 70%.
No model was trained, so these values are not benchmark scores.

## Limits

This is an E. coli K-12 pilot, not an all-bacteria benchmark. MMseqs2's fixed search is heuristic,
and no-match does not prove biological independence. The release makes no claim about classifier
accuracy, feature quality, or the effect of split strategy on model performance.
