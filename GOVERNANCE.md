# Project governance

## Roles

Aria Chen (legal name Jiajie Chen) is the current project maintainer. Contributors may propose code, documentation,
protocol, and reproducibility changes through public issues and pull requests. Review participation
does not by itself grant release or protocol-approval authority.

## Decisions

Ordinary implementation decisions are made through review of tests, documentation, compatibility,
and maintenance cost. Decisions that change a frozen cohort, split, model matrix, Test gate,
statistical analysis, public artifact allowlist, or scientific claim require an explicit protocol
revision and permanent maintainer approval before formal execution.

Historical releases and attestations are immutable. Corrections are additive and must explain the
relationship to the original evidence. A failed formal attempt remains part of the audit history.

## Releases

Only the maintainer may authorize a commit, tag, package publication, or GitHub Release. Release
authorization is scoped to the reviewed commit and artifact hashes; a content change or failed
required check invalidates that authorization.

## Participation and succession

Contributors who make sustained, reviewed contributions may be invited to take on triage or review
responsibilities. Transfer or expansion of maintainer authority must be recorded publicly before
it takes effect. If maintenance stops, the repository should state that status rather than imply
active support.
